Caveats

  • Cumulus has only six seasons of hindcasts to test on; one unusual season can change the ranking.
  • The live ECMWF forecast has 101 members, but its weights were trained on a reforecast with 10.
  • CHIRPS confirms an onset only three weeks after the first rains, so the most recent starts are not marked yet.
  • Only two weeks ahead, because the onset rule looks 27 days past the first rains.
